    The road to the platinum

    Six stages, built from the official descriptions: story chapters first, then the stranger strands, then activities and the one missable, then collectibles, then robberies and money, and 100% completion last. The platinum pops when the other 54 are done.

      Missable trophies

      Only one trophy on this list can be missed. Everything else can be finished in free roam after the story, because nothing in its official description ties it to a single story moment.

      Save advice

      There is no mission replay in GTA VI, so a manual save is your only way back. Keep a save at the start of every chapter and one more before the final mission. That single habit protects the one missable trophy and every story trophy alike.

      About & sources

      Leonida Ledger is an offline guide and tracker for the 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI. It loads nothing from the network: every file it needs sits under app/, and progress is saved only in this browser's local storage.

      Where each fact comes from

      • Names, descriptions, grades and hidden flags — the official PlayStation list as published on Exophase — GTA VI trophy list. Exophase and PSNProfiles agree on every name, description and grade.
      • PlayStation progress targets (such as 0/42 Shooting Range challenges) — PSNProfiles — GTA VI trophy list.
      • The one missable trophy and the no-mission-replay warning — Output Lag — GTA 6 trophy list; GTA Vibes — GTA 6 trophy list.
      • Every guide is written in our own words for this guide, from the official description. Where a description does not pin a trigger down, the guide says so instead of guessing. A guide that repeats the trophy's description adds nothing, so each one says what to do, where and how, and when it can be missed.

      How to use it

      • Tick a trophy when you earn it — progress saves automatically and survives closing the browser.
      • Export downloads your progress as a JSON file; Import reads one back.
      • Turn on “Hide story spoilers” to keep story trophy names, descriptions and guides hidden until you earn them.
      • Follow the Platinum roadmap in order: story, strangers, activities, collectibles, robberies, then 100% completion.
